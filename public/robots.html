Overview & Indexing Guidelines

This document outlines the crawler directives, bot policies, and indexing permissions for Compassionate Love of Calvary Ministries (CLM). All major search engines (including Google, Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, Baidu, and Yandex) are welcome to index our public ministry content, devotionals, events, sermons, and articles.

✔ Allowed Public Routes

Crawlers are permitted and encouraged to crawl and index all public ministry pages:

  • / (Home Page)
  • /about (About Ministry)
  • /ministries (Ministries & Outreach)
  • /bible (Holy Bible)
  • /study (Bible Study)
  • /devotional (Daily Devotions)
  • /sermons (Sermons & Media)
  • /events (Church Events)
  • /blog (Articles & News)
  • /media (Gallery & Videos)
  • /contact (Location & Inquiries)

✖ Restricted & Protected Routes

Private consoles, authentication portals, and backend APIs must NOT be indexed:

  • /admin (Admin Console)
  • /admin/* (All Admin Editors)
  • /adminLogin (Authentication Portal)
  • /api/* (Internal REST Endpoints)
  • /django-admin/* (Backend Administration)
